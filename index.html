Welcome to Metric

Your beginner-friendly guide to the Metric DEX and Liquidity Pools.

Core Products & How to Participate

Liquidity

1. Active Pools

What it is: Liquidity bins that automatically move with external oracle prices.

Why use it: No need to manually adjust your price range. Your capital stays active near the market price.

How to earn: Earn the bid-ask spread and trading fees.

⚠ Risk: Inventory risk if asset prices drop; oracle lag.

Trading

2. Swapping

What it is: Exchange tokens directly using Metric's liquidity.

Feature: Quotes include the spread. Uses oracle pricing for accuracy.

Tip: Look for the blue star route to earn points!

Earning

3. Liquidity Provision

How to do it: Select a pool (like WETH/USDC), deposit assets, and get a position token.

Rewards: Fees compound automatically in the bins.

⚠ Warning: Check "Markout APR" before depositing. High APR doesn't always mean profit.

Advanced

4. Self-Deployment

For pros: Create private pools or act as a curator for public pools.

Feature: Customize pricing logic and integrate specific oracles (like Pyth).

Incentives

5. Points Program

What it is: Weekly distribution of 1 million points to users.

Eligibility: Trade, provide liquidity, or invite others.

Note: Distributed every Thursday. Potential airdrop credential.
